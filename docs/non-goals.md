# Non-Goals

- No detection of AI usage.
- No inference or evaluation of correctness or quality.
- No attestations, certifications, or audit artifacts.
- No governance, policy enforcement, or blocking behavior.
- No aggregation, reporting, scoring, or summaries.
- No responsibility assignment or approval workflows.
- No network calls, telemetry, or remote configuration.

Any feature that implies the above is out of scope and forbidden.
